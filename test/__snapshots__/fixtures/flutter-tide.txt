How to fix

[A]      Stop overriding the text scaler for the whole app
         1. Remove the MediaQuery, MediaQuery.withNoTextScaling or TextScaler.noScaling that wraps
            the app in MaterialApp.builder.
         2. If a limit is needed, use MediaQuery.withClampedTextScaling with maxScaleFactor 2.0 or
            more, or clamp only the widgets that cannot grow.
         3. Check the common tasks at the largest accessibility size on an iOS simulator.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/larger-text.md#flutter

[B]      Let this text scale
         1. Remove textScaler: TextScaler.noScaling (or linear(1.0), or textScaleFactor: 1.0) from
            this widget.
         2. If it cannot grow, clamp it with MediaQuery.withClampedTextScaling(maxScaleFactor: 2.0)
            rather than turning scaling off.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/larger-text.md#flutter

[C]      Add a dark theme
         1. Pass darkTheme: ThemeData(colorScheme: ColorScheme.fromSeed(seedColor: …, brightness:
            Brightness.dark)) to MaterialApp.
         2. Leave themeMode at ThemeMode.system, and read colours from Theme.of(context).colorScheme
            instead of constants.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/dark-interface.md#flutter

[D]      Read Reduce Motion and calm the animation
         1. Read PlatformDispatcher.instance.accessibilityFeatures.reduceMotion (iOS) together with
            MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context) (Android).
         2. When either is set, do not call repeat() or auto-play Lottie and carousels; jump to the
            end value instead.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/reduced-motion.md#flutter

[E]      Also read the iOS setting
         1. Combine both: MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context) ||
            View.of(context).platformDispatcher.accessibilityFeatures.reduceMotion.
         2. Rebuild when the setting changes: listen to
            PlatformDispatcher.onAccessibilityFeaturesChanged or
            WidgetsBindingObserver.didChangeAccessibilityFeatures.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/reduced-motion.md#flutter

[F]      Name the button
         1. Pass tooltip: 'Close' to IconButton or FloatingActionButton; Flutter uses it as the
            semantic label.
         2. For other buttons, give the Icon a semanticLabel or wrap the button in Semantics(label:
            …, button: true).
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/a11ylabel/blob/main/docs/guides/voiceover.md#flutter
